What it does

  • Rent roll: 12 months x up to 10 units. Type the amount and date received; each month is marked Paid, Late, Partial, Unpaid, Vacant or Upcoming automatically
  • Your rules: set the rent due day and how many days after that rent counts as late
  • Arrears: rent still owed per unit and in total, plus a late count per tenant
  • Leases: lease status and days left for every unit; leases ending in the next 60 days (your choice) are listed on the dashboard
  • Rent increases: enter the new rent and the date it starts; the rent due updates from that month
  • Expense log: date, property, category dropdown, payee, amount. Shared costs (e.g. your accountant) go to "General / shared"
  • Property P&L: rent collected, expenses by category, net income, yield on purchase price, gross yield, occupancy, arrears and deposits held, per property and in total
  • Dashboard: year-to-date income, expenses, net, rent owed, occupancy, deposits, rent status by unit, month-by-month income vs expenses chart and expenses by category chart
  • Any tracking year (calendar year or your own start month) and any currency label

What's included

One .xlsx file with 6 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Setup (6 properties, 10 units, 15 editable expense categories), Rent Roll, Expenses (300 rows), Property P&L. An example portfolio (3 properties, 4 units) is filled in so you can see how it works; clear it when you're ready.

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.

Important

The expense categories are generic groupings modelled on common rental expense types to keep your records tidy. This is a record-keeping tool, not tax, legal or investment advice, and the categories may not match your tax authority's forms. Ask a qualified tax professional how to report rental income and expenses where you live.

FAQ

  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • More than 10 units? This version is built for 1-10 units.
  • Does it prorate rent for part months? No. Rent is due in each month where a lease covers the due day; any rent you do receive in a part month is still counted.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it is easiest to use on a computer.
