What it does

  • Clients (150 rows): every inquiry with date, couple, event type, event date, venue, lead source and status (Inquiry, Quoted, Booked, Lost). Pick a package and up to 2 add-ons (plus travel fee or discount) to get the quote / contract total. For booked couples it shows paid to date, balance due, the next payment date, any overdue amount and days to the event. Alerts: Payment overdue (red), Schedule payments when the plan doesn't add up to the total (amber), Follow up on open inquiries after your chosen number of days (amber)
  • Payments (400 rows): retainer, instalments and final balance with due dates. Type the date when the money arrives. Overdue = red, due within 14 days (your choice) = amber, paid = green
  • Workflow (100 weddings): a 7-step checklist per wedding (contract, questionnaire, timeline, shot list, sneak peek, gallery delivered, thank-you & review request). Each step's deadline is the event date plus your own timing (e.g. gallery 60 days after). Overdue steps turn red, due-soon steps amber; shows the gallery due date, progress and the next step
  • CODB Pricing: enter your yearly business costs, costs per wedding, the salary you want, tax set-aside, card fees, weddings per year and the hours one wedding really takes (editing included). You get your minimum price per wedding (rounded up), your real hourly rate, a reality check of your average booking value against that minimum, and a check of every package: profit, hourly rate and the minimum it should cost for its hours
  • Dashboard: booked revenue (by event month) and money collected (by payment month), outstanding and overdue totals, bookings, average booking value, conversion rate, next year's bookings, upcoming events with their next workflow step, payments to chase, lead sources with conversion, a month-by-month table and 2 charts
  • Settings: your packages (coverage hours, price, total work hours), add-ons, workflow steps and timings, lead sources, event types, payment types and methods. Any currency label

What's included

One .xlsx file with 7 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Clients, Payments, Workflow, CODB Pricing, Settings. An example photography business (39 inquiries, 25 bookings, 68 payments, a season of workflow) is filled in so you can see how it works; clear it when you're ready.

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.

Important

The minimum price comes from the costs, salary and hours you enter. It is a floor for your own business, not market advice: check it against what couples in your area pay. This is a business record and pricing tool, not tax, legal or financial advice.

FAQ

  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • Is this a full CRM? No. It does not send emails, contracts, invoices or reminders, and it does not take payments. It shows who owes what, what is overdue and what to do next; you contact couples your usual way.
  • Can I use my own packages, add-ons and workflow steps? Yes: up to 8 packages, 8 add-ons and 7 workflow steps with your own names and timings, all on the Settings tab.
  • I also shoot engagements and portraits. Can I track them? Yes, pick the event type (Wedding, Elopement, Engagement, Portrait / family, Other). The CODB calculator is built around weddings.
  • Does it work in Apple Numbers? Not supported. Use Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it is easiest to use on a computer.
