What it does

  • Appointments (1,500 rows): pick the date, the pet (dropdown from your client list), the service, up to 2 add-ons and Salon or Mobile. The price fills in from your price list for that dog's size (Small, Medium, Large, XL), plus add-ons and your mobile travel fee, minus any discount. Add the tip, payment method, status (Booked, Done, No-show, Cancelled), Paid or Unpaid and the minutes it took. You get revenue, sales tax, total paid, estimated supplies, the card / app fee, your net and your net per hour (red when it's below your target). A check column flags rows with a missing pet, size, service or price
  • Clients & Pets (200 rows): owner, phone, email, pet name, breed, size, coat notes, temperament and rabies vaccine expiry. Last visit, next due date (last visit + the rebook interval in weeks), next booking, visits, revenue this year and average ticket fill in from your appointments. Rabies: expired = red, due within 30 days = amber, OK = green, missing = purple. Rebook: overdue = red, due within 14 days = amber, already booked = green
  • Dashboard: revenue and appointments this month, revenue and net profit this year, average ticket, what you earn per grooming hour, tips, unpaid appointments, rebooks due, vaccines to check, rebook rate and bookings in the next 7 days. Plus a rebook call list (overdue first, with phone numbers), a list of rabies vaccines to check, revenue by service, a month-by-month table and 2 charts (income vs expenses by month, revenue by service)
  • Monthly P&L (January to December): appointments, no-shows, service revenue, tips, payment fees, expenses by category, net profit, margin, average ticket, grooming hours, net per hour, rebook rate, and memo lines for sales tax collected and estimated supplies used
  • Expenses (400 rows): shampoo & supplies, van & fuel, equipment, insurance, rent, marketing, software and your own categories
  • Settings: your price list by dog size (up to 12 services), add-ons with price and supply cost, payment methods with their fees (e.g. card 2.6% + 0.15), sales tax rate, mobile travel fee, target per hour, default rebook interval and the "due soon" windows

What's included

One .xlsx file with 7 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Appointments, Clients & Pets, Expenses, Monthly P&L, Settings. An example grooming business (6 services, 7 add-ons, 26 dogs, about 90 appointments and its expenses) is filled in so you can see how it works; clear it when you're ready.

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.
  • Every price, size price, add-on, fee and category is editable.

Important

The example prices, minutes and costs are placeholders, not pricing advice: grooming prices vary a lot by region. Check the sales tax rules for your area (set 0% if grooming isn't taxed). This is a record-keeping tool, not tax, legal or veterinary advice. Net profit is before income tax and before paying yourself.

FAQ

  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • Does it send reminders to my clients? No. It shows who is due or overdue (with their phone number) and whose rabies vaccine needs checking; you contact clients your usual way.
  • Is it a booking calendar? No, it's a log: one row per appointment. Booked appointments show the expected price and count towards "booked next 7 days" and rebook status.
  • What is "net per hour"? Revenue plus tip, minus estimated supplies and the payment fee, divided by the minutes you spent: what one hour on the table brings in. Compare it with the target you set.
  • Are supplies counted twice? No. Supply costs per service are estimates for the per-appointment net; the Monthly P&L uses the real expenses you log (plus payment fees).
  • I'm mobile only / salon only. Does it work? Yes. Choose Salon or Mobile on each appointment; the travel fee is only added on Mobile.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it is easiest to use on a computer.
  • Apple Numbers? Not supported; use Excel or Google Sheets.
