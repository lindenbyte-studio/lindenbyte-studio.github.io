What it does

  • Quote Calculator: pick the vehicle size, the service and up to 4 add-ons, type the distance, a condition surcharge (heavy soil, pet hair) and any discount. You get the price with travel fee and sales tax, never below your minimum job charge, plus the estimated hours, product cost, card fee and round-trip mileage, your profit and profit per hour (red when it's below your target) and the price you would need to hit your target
  • Jobs (1,000 rows): date, client and vehicle (dropdown), service, add-ons, status (Quoted, Booked, Done, Paid, Cancelled), tip, payment method and hours. The price fills in from your price list for that vehicle's size; type a price override for custom quotes. You get revenue, sales tax, total paid, payment fee, product, mileage, net profit and profit per hour. Done-but-unpaid jobs are flagged, and a check column catches missing sizes, services or prices
  • Clients & Vehicles (200 rows): client, phone, email, vehicle, size, distance and rebook interval (e.g. every 2, 4 or 8 weeks for maintenance plans). Last service, next due date, next booking, jobs, revenue and average ticket fill in from your jobs. Rebook overdue = red, due soon = amber, booked = green. Coated vehicles get a ceramic top-up due date from the last coating or boost
  • Dashboard: revenue, jobs and net profit this month, bookings in the next 7 days, revenue and net profit over the last 12 months, average ticket, profit per labour hour, repeat clients, unpaid jobs, rebooks due, ceramic top-ups due, tax set-aside, open quotes, business miles and mileage. Plus a rebook call list with phone numbers, a ceramic top-up list, revenue by service, a month-by-month table and 2 charts
  • Monthly P&L (rolling 12 months): jobs, revenue, tips, payment fees, expenses by category, net profit, margin, average ticket, labour hours, profit per hour, income tax set-aside, and memo lines for sales tax, product used, miles driven and mileage at your rate
  • Expenses (400 rows): chemicals, equipment, fuel, van, insurance, marketing, software, water & power, licenses and your own categories
  • Settings: price list for 4 vehicle sizes (price AND hours per size, product per job, up to 12 services), up to 12 add-ons (price, extra time, product), payment methods with their fees, sales tax, free travel radius and fee per mile or km, minimum charge, vehicle cost per mile or km, tax set-aside %, hourly target and reminder intervals

What's included

One .xlsx file with 8 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Quote Calculator, Jobs, Clients & Vehicles, Expenses, Monthly P&L, Settings. How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.
  • Every price, size name, add-on, fee and category is editable. Works with miles or km and any currency label.

Important

The example prices, hours and costs are placeholders, not pricing advice: detailing prices vary a lot by region. Check the sales tax rules for your area (set 0% if detailing isn't taxed). This is a pricing and record-keeping tool, not tax or legal advice. The tax set-aside is a simple percentage you choose, not a tax calculation. Net profit is before income tax and before paying yourself.

FAQ

  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • Does it send reminders to my clients? No. It shows who is due or overdue for a rebook or a ceramic top-up (with their phone number); you contact clients your usual way.
  • Is it a booking calendar? No, it's a log: one row per quote or job. Booked jobs show the expected price and count towards "booked next 7 days" and rebook status.
  • What is "profit per hour"? Revenue plus tip, minus the payment fee, estimated product and mileage, divided by the hours the job took: what one hour of your work brings in. Compare it with the target you set.
  • Are product and fuel counted twice? No. Product and mileage per job are estimates for the per-job profit; the Monthly P&L uses the real expenses you log (plus payment fees) and shows the estimates as memo lines.
  • How is the travel fee worked out? (Distance one way - your free radius) x your fee per mile or km, never below zero. Set the fee to 0 if you don't charge travel.
  • Can I rename the vehicle sizes? Yes. Change the 4 size names on Settings (for example Compact, SUV, Truck, Van) and the dropdowns follow.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it is easiest to use on a computer.
  • Apple Numbers? Not supported; use Excel or Google Sheets.
