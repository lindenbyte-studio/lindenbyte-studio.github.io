Keep your renovation on budget, from the first quote to the final payment. Compare contractor bids, log every change order and see exactly how much money is left, what's due next and whether your contingency is holding.

What it does

  • Dashboard: total budget, projected cost, paid so far, money left, contingency left, approved changes, still to pay and % of budget used, plus 3 charts
  • Contractor quote comparison: up to 3 bids per job side by side. The lowest bid is highlighted and the price spread calculated; pick the bid you accept and its price flows into your budget automatically
  • Change orders: log extras, hidden surprises, code requirements and credits. Approved changes are added to the job's projected cost; pending ones show as a warning so you can decide before you commit
  • Contingency tracking: set your reserve % (e.g. 15%) and see how much of it overruns and changes have used
  • Payment schedule: deposits, progress payments and final payments turn red when overdue, amber when due soon (you choose the window, 14 days by default) and green when paid. The next 5 payments are listed on the dashboard
  • Paid-ahead check: enter % complete per job and get a warning if you've paid a contractor more than the work done so far
  • Cash plan: payments by month from your start month, with the budget left after each month, so you know when the money has to be there
  • Budget by category: rooms or trades (your choice), with over-budget and near-limit flags
  • Any currency: type your own currency label

What's included

One .xlsx file with 7 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Setup (14 budget categories), Costs (100 rows), Quotes (50 jobs), Change Orders (50 rows), Payments (100 rows). An example kitchen & bathroom remodel is filled in so you can see how everything connects; clear it when you're ready.

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.

Important

This is a budgeting and record-keeping tool. It doesn't give financial, legal or construction advice and doesn't replace your written contracts. The example project and prices are made up for illustration.

FAQ

  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • Is it only for kitchens and bathrooms? No. Rename the categories to fit any project: a single room, a whole-house remodel, an extension or a flip.
  • I'm a contractor, can I use it for clients? It's designed for the homeowner's side (quotes received, payments made). Contractors may prefer a job-costing tool.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it's easiest to use on a computer.
