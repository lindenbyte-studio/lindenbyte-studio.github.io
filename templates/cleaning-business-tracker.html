What it does

  • Quote Calculator: enter square feet, rooms (bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, living areas), service type, frequency, add-ons and driving distance. You get estimated labour hours, time on site for your crew, a room-based quote AND a square-foot quote, the suggested price, profit after supplies and travel, and your price and profit per hour. 3 columns let you compare 3 homes or 3 options (e.g. standard vs deep clean)
  • Price Book: your target hourly rate, minutes per room type, price per sq ft, minimum job price, rounding, add-ons (price + extra minutes), service multipliers (deep clean, move-in/move-out, post-renovation), frequency discounts (weekly, biweekly, every 3 weeks, monthly, one-off), supplies cost % and travel cost per mile or km. Every tab reads from it
  • Clients: up to 60 clients with frequency and status. The next due clean is calculated from the last clean in your job log; overdue clients turn red, clients due in the next 7 days (your choice) turn amber, booked visits turn green. Also shows each client's jobs, revenue, real hourly rate and unpaid balance this year, so you can spot who you are undercharging
  • Jobs log (1,000 rows): date, client dropdown, hours actually worked, price, miles, paid or unpaid. Profit per job, real hourly rate, unpaid amount and overdue payments are calculated
  • Expenses: overhead log with category dropdown (insurance, equipment, marketing, software and more)
  • Dashboard: revenue, expenses, net profit, real hourly rate, jobs, hours, unpaid total, clients to rebook, top 5 clients, a month-by-month table and 2 charts
  • Any currency label, miles or km

What's included

One .xlsx file with 7 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Quote Calculator, Price Book, Clients, Jobs, Expenses. An example cleaning business (16 clients, a year of jobs) is filled in so you can see how it works; clear it when you're ready.

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.

Important

The suggested prices come from the rates and times you enter. They are a starting point, not market advice: check them against what clients in your area pay. This is a pricing and record-keeping tool, not tax or legal advice.

FAQ

  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • I have a helper. Does it work for teams? Yes: enter the number of cleaners on a quote to see time on site, and log total labour hours on each job. It is designed for solo cleaners and small teams, not for payroll.
  • Can I use my own room types, add-ons and discounts? Yes, rename and edit them on the Price Book (6 room types, 8 add-ons, 5 service types, 6 frequencies).
  • Does it send invoices or reminders? No. It shows who is unpaid and who is due for a clean; you contact clients your usual way.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it is easiest to use on a computer.
