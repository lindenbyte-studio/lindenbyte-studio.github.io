What it does

  • Booking log: listing and channel dropdowns, check-in and check-out dates, rate, cleaning fee. Nights, accommodation revenue, platform fee and payout are calculated for every stay
  • Your fee rates: set the fee % for each channel once (Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, Direct, Other). Know the exact fee from your payout report? Type it and it replaces the estimate
  • Taxes you collect from guests are kept in their own column and not counted as income
  • Arrivals: check-ins in the next 14 days (your choice) are highlighted, "Arrives today", "Staying now" and "Leaves today" shown automatically
  • Same-day turnovers flagged (a guest checks out the day the next one arrives), and two bookings of the same listing sharing a night are flagged as a double booking
  • Stays across month-end are split correctly: a stay from 29 Jan to 2 Feb counts 3 nights in January and 1 in February
  • Expense log: date, listing (or Shared), category dropdown, payee, amount. 11 editable categories (cleaning, laundry, supplies, utilities, mortgage/rent and more)
  • Monthly P&L: pick one listing or all of them from a dropdown and see 12 months of nights available, booked nights, occupancy, check-ins, average stay, ADR, RevPAR, payout, expenses and net profit. Below it, every listing side by side
  • Dashboard: year-to-date payout, expenses, net profit, future payouts already booked, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, the next check-ins with turnovers flagged, results per listing, channel mix, net profit by month chart and payout by channel chart
  • A listing that went live mid-year? Enter its start date and occupancy only counts the nights it was available
  • Any currency label and any tracking-year start month

What's included

One .xlsx file with 7 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Settings (5 listings, 5 channels, 15 expense categories), Bookings (500 rows), Expenses (400 rows), Monthly P&L, Nights by Month (automatic). An example business with 3 listings and a year of stays is filled in so you can see how it works; clear it when you're ready.

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.

About the fee defaults

The Airbnb default is 15.5%, the host-only fee Airbnb's help centre lists for most hosts (checked November 2026; hosts still on the split fee mostly pay 3%). The Vrbo and Booking.com rates are examples. Fees differ by plan and country, so set the rate you actually pay.

FAQ

  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • Does it connect to my Airbnb account? No. You type or paste your bookings; nothing is linked to any platform.
  • More than 5 listings? This version is built for 1-5 listings.
  • When is payout counted? In the month of check-in. Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR use the nights actually stayed in each month.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it is easiest to use on a computer.
