Find out what you really keep from every Etsy sale. Log your orders and costs, and this spreadsheet works out every Etsy fee, your net profit per order and per month, and the price you need to charge to hit your target margin.

What it does

  • Every Etsy fee per order: listing, transaction, payment processing, Offsite Ads, currency conversion
  • All fee rates are editable, pre-filled with Etsy's US rates (checked November 2026); change them for your country or when Etsy updates its fees
  • Pricing Calculator: fee breakdown for a normal sale and an Offsite Ads sale, break-even price, price for your target margin and a price ladder (80% to 150% of your price)
  • Products tab: unit cost from materials, packaging and your time, profit per sale and margin with colour flags
  • Monthly P&L: orders, units, revenue, Etsy fees, shipping labels, materials, overheads, net profit and margin for 12 months, plus "profit after paying yourself"
  • Dashboard: year totals, average order, fees as % of revenue, top 5 products, monthly revenue vs profit chart and a chart of where your fees go
  • Handles discounts, multi-item orders (one fixed processing fee per order), sales tax collected by Etsy and Offsite Ads orders

What's included

One .xlsx file with 8 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Fee Settings, Products (50 rows), Pricing Calculator, Orders (500 rows), Expenses (300 rows, 14 editable categories), Monthly P&L. An example candle shop is filled in so you can see how everything works; delete it when you're ready (Start Here shows how).

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates. No macros, nothing locked.

FAQ

  • Are the fees exact? They follow Etsy's published rates and round each fee to the cent, like Etsy does, but fees vary by country and change over time. Check the Fee Settings tab against Etsy's fee page; your Etsy payment account is always the final word.
  • I'm not in the US. Type your own rates on the Fee Settings tab (e.g. payment processing differs by country) and your currency label.
  • Is this tax or accounting advice? No. It is a bookkeeping and pricing tool. Ask a local accountant how to report your income.
  • Does it import my Etsy CSV? No, you type or paste your orders. Most small shops need a few minutes a week.
  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
