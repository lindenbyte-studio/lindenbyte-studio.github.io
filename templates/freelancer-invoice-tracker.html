What it does

  • Invoice log (300 rows): the due date fills in from each client's payment terms (or type your own). Tax, total, balance and days overdue are calculated, and each invoice is marked Paid, Overdue, Due soon, Partially paid, Open or Draft automatically: red, amber and green at a glance
  • Part payments and drafts handled: type what you've received so far, or leave the issue date empty until you send it
  • Aging report: what each client owes, split into Current / 1-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 90+ days past due
  • Clients (30): rate, terms, invoiced, collected, outstanding, overdue and average days to pay, so you know who pays slowly
  • Time log (optional): hours x rate (client rate or a custom one), with a running total of work you haven't billed yet
  • Expenses (300 rows) with 9 ready-made categories, all editable, room for 12
  • Monthly summary (cash basis): invoiced, collected, how much of that was sales tax / VAT, expenses, profit, a tax set-aside at the % you choose, and what's left that's safe to spend
  • Dashboard: 12 key numbers, top clients, overdue invoices oldest first, invoiced vs collected chart and revenue by client chart
  • Sales tax / VAT / GST: set your default % (0% if you don't charge it) and change it on any invoice, e.g. 0% for a client you don't charge tax to. Tax you charge is kept out of your income and profit
  • Any currency label and any tracking year (calendar year or your own start month)

What's included

One .xlsx file with 9 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Settings, Clients, Invoices, Aging, Time Log, Expenses, Monthly Summary. An example design studio is filled in so you can see how everything works; clear the shaded cells when you're ready.

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.

IMPORTANT: NOT TAX ADVICE The tax set-aside is a simple savings estimate (your chosen % of each month's profit). It is not a tax calculation. The spreadsheet does not know your country's tax rules, and it does not tell you which sales tax / VAT rate applies to which client: you type the rates. Expense categories are generic groupings, not official tax categories. This is a record-keeping tool, not tax, legal or accounting advice. Ask a qualified accountant what you owe and how to report it.

FAQ

  • Does it create the invoices I send to clients? No. It tracks invoices you send from anywhere (your own template, Word, an invoicing app) and tells you what's paid, due and overdue.
  • I bill clients in different currencies. Totals add up amounts as typed, so type invoice amounts converted into your main currency and keep the original amount in Notes.
  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it is easiest to use on a computer.
