What it does

  • Quote Calculator: type the lot size and the area that isn't lawn (house, drive, beds); the lawn area fills in by itself on per-1,000 sq ft services. Add up to 12 service lines (mowing, edging, trimming, blow-off, leaf cleanup, mulch, aeration, overseeding, fertilizer, hedges, gutters, haul-away...) and mark each as Every visit or One-time. Pick the frequency (one-time, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly). You get the price per visit after the recurring discount (never below your minimum job charge), the monthly recurring value, the season value, one-time work, sales tax and the total, plus labour hours, time on site for your crew, labour + equipment cost, materials, drive cost, gross profit, margin, effective hourly rate (On target / Below target) and the price per visit you would need to hit your target rate
  • Price Book: your labour cost per hour, target hourly rate, equipment cost per hour, vehicle cost per mile, minimum job charge, sales tax, setup minutes, season length and payment terms; frequencies with visits per month and recurring discounts; up to 16 services, each with unit, rate, minutes per unit, material cost and material markup. Calculated columns show each service's price, cost, margin, effective hourly rate and this year's jobs and revenue
  • Clients & Routes (100 rows): recurring clients with route day, frequency, price per visit, minutes on site and drive miles. Effective hourly rate (after drive cost), cost and profit per visit, margin, monthly and season revenue and revenue this year are calculated; clients below your target rate turn red, so you know whom to re-price or move to another day
  • Job Log (500 rows): quotes, scheduled jobs and invoices with status Quoted, Scheduled, Done, Invoiced, Paid or Lost. Due date = job date + your payment terms (or type your own, e.g. net 30). Overdue invoices turn red, invoices due within 14 days amber, paid green; finished jobs not invoiced yet show "To invoice"
  • Expenses & Mileage (300 rows each): expenses by category (fuel, equipment, repairs, materials, dump fees, insurance, marketing, wages and more) and a business mileage log with the vehicle cost of each trip
  • Dashboard: revenue, expenses, profit and margin this year, outstanding invoices (and how many are overdue), work done but not invoiced, open quotes, quote win rate, average effective hourly rate, clients below target, monthly recurring revenue, business miles, top clients, routes by day, expenses by category, a month-by-month table and 2 charts (revenue vs expenses by month, expenses by category)

One .xlsx file with 7 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Quote Calculator, Price Book, Clients & Routes, Job Log, Expenses & Mileage. How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.
  • US units (sq ft, linear ft, cubic yards, miles). Every unit, rate and minute value is editable.

Important

The example rates, minutes and costs are placeholders, not pricing advice: lawn care prices vary a lot by region. Prices come from the rates and costs you enter, so check them against what customers in your area pay. This is a pricing and record-keeping tool, not tax or legal advice; check the sales tax rules for your area (set 0% if your services aren't taxed). Profit is before income tax and before paying yourself if you are the owner.

FAQ

  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • What is the "effective hourly rate"? Price minus materials and drive cost, divided by the labour hours (including your setup minutes): what one hour of work brings in before labour and equipment costs. Compare it with the target you set.
  • How does the recurring pricing work? Every-visit lines make the visit price; the frequency's discount is applied (e.g. 10% weekly). Monthly value = price per visit x visits per month (4.33 for weekly); season value = price per visit x visits per season (visits per month x your season months). One-time work is priced on top.
  • Can I price per hour or per job instead of per square foot? Yes: each service has its own unit (the example has per 1,000 sq ft, linear ft, cubic yard, hour, visit, job and load).
  • I work with a crew. Does it work? Yes: enter the crew size on a quote to see time on site, and enter minutes on site for all crew together on Clients & Routes. It is not a payroll tool.
  • Does it send invoices or reminders? No. It shows what is due, overdue and to invoice; you contact clients your usual way.
  • Does it plan the driving route? No, it groups clients by route day and shows each day's revenue and effective hourly rate.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it is easiest to use on a computer.
  • Apple Numbers? Not supported; use Excel or Google Sheets.
