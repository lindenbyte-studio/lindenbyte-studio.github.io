Plan your wedding budget without the spreadsheet headache. Enter your total budget and guest count, log quotes and payments, and the planner tells you what's paid, what's due next and where you're going over.

What it does

  • Dashboard: total budget, projected total, paid so far, remaining, % used, cost per guest, plus 2 charts
  • Due-date alerts: every deposit and instalment turns red when overdue, amber when due soon (you pick the window, 14 days by default), green when paid
  • Next 5 payments listed on the dashboard so nothing slips
  • Smart budget split: 16 categories with a suggested split you can override; over-budget and near-limit flags
  • Guest-count what-ifs: see what 20 more (or fewer) guests really cost
  • Any currency: type your own currency label

What's included

One .xlsx file with 6 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Setup, Expenses (100 rows), Payments (100 rows), Guest Scenarios. An example wedding is filled in so you can see how everything works; delete it in one step when you're ready.

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). Free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.
