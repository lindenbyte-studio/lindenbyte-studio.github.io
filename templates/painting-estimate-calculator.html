What it does

  • Quote Builder, interior: one row per room. Enter length, width, height, doors, windows, coats, ceiling yes/no, trim yes/no and a prep difficulty. Wall area (minus door and window openings), ceiling area, trim length, painting hours and paint coat area are calculated for you
  • Quote Builder, exterior: pick the surface (siding, stucco, brick, trim and fascia, doors, deck) and type the square footage. Rough surfaces use a coverage factor, so stucco needs more paint than smooth siding
  • Paint to buy: pick a product for walls, ceilings, trim and exterior. You get the gallons needed (coats, coverage per gallon and waste allowance included), the whole gallons to buy and the paint cost
  • Price: labour hours x your labour cost, paint, other materials, other job costs, overhead %, markup % (with a per-job override), discount, minimum job charge, rounding, sales tax, deposit due at booking and balance due. Also gross profit, margin, net profit after overhead, estimated hourly rate and an On target / Below target check, plus working days on site for your crew
  • Price Book: your labour cost, setup hours, overhead, markup, target margin, minimum charge, deposit, waste %, production speeds (sq ft per hour), paint products (price per gallon, coverage), prep multipliers, exterior surfaces and job types
  • Job Log (1,000 rows): every quote from Quoted to Booked to Completed (or Lost). After the job, type actual hours, actual paint and materials and other costs: profit, margin, real hourly rate and flags (over hours, over cost, faster than estimate) are calculated. Quotes waiting too long say "Follow up", jobs in the next 14 days say "Upcoming", unpaid jobs past your payment window turn red
  • Expenses: overhead log with category dropdown
  • Dashboard: revenue, job profit, net profit after overhead, gross margin, jobs completed, average job value, real hourly rate, quote close rate, unpaid total, quotes to follow up, open quotes value, upcoming jobs, most profitable job types, a month-by-month table and 2 charts
  • Any currency label

What's included

One .xlsx file with 6 connected tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Quote Builder, Price Book, Job Log, Expenses. An example painting company (a 7-room house estimate with exterior trim, a year of quotes and jobs) is filled in so you can see how it works; clear it when you're ready.

How it works

  • Excel: open the file (Excel 2010 or newer, Windows or Mac).
  • Google Sheets: in Google Drive choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets (or File > Import). A free Google account is enough.
  • Shaded cells are yours to edit; everything else calculates automatically. No macros, nothing locked.

Important

The example rates, production speeds and paint prices are placeholders, not market advice: they vary a lot by region, crew and product. Replace them with your own numbers (the can tells you the coverage). This is a pricing and record-keeping tool, not tax or legal advice; check the sales tax rules for your area (set 0% if your services aren't taxed).

FAQ

  • Is anything physical shipped? No, this is a digital download only.
  • How is the paint quantity worked out? Wall area = 2 x (length + width) x height minus doors and windows. Gallons = area x coats / coverage per gallon, plus your waste %, rounded up to whole gallons. Ceilings and trim are done the same way with their own paint.
  • What is the "real hourly rate"? Price minus paint, materials and other job costs, divided by the hours worked: what one hour of painting earned before paying anyone. Gross profit and margin also subtract your labour cost per hour.
  • I work with a helper. Does it work for crews? Yes: enter the number of painters on a quote to see time on site and working days, and log total labour hours (all crew together) on each job. It is not a payroll tool.
  • Can I use my own paints, surfaces and job types? Yes, rename and edit them on the Price Book (10 paint rows, 5 prep levels, 8 exterior surfaces, 8 job types, 12 expense categories).
  • Does it send quotes, invoices or reminders? No. It calculates the price and shows who to follow up with and who hasn't paid; you contact customers your usual way.
  • Does it work on a phone? It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it is easiest to use on a computer.
  • Apple Numbers? Not supported; use Excel or Google Sheets.
